Español

Tu correo está confirmado. Vuelve a ATHENA e inicia sesión con tu correo y tu contraseña. Ya puedes cerrar esta página.

English

Your email address is confirmed. Go back to ATHENA and sign in with your email and password. You can close this page now.

If the link had expired (it lasts one hour) or you had already used it, the app will tell you that your email is not confirmed yet when you sign in. If so, create the account again with the same email address and we will send you a new message.
