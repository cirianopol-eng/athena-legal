Delete your ATHENA account

This page is available in Spanish, English and German. All three versions are equally valid.

ATHENA is an app by Pol Ciriano Marteles. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data.

From the app

  • Open ATHENA and sign in.
  • Go to Settings ⚙️ → Account & cloud → Delete my account.
  • Enter your password and confirm.

It is deleted straight away. If you want to keep a copy, download your data first (Settings ⚙️ → Account & cloud → Download my data).

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, write to us from your account's email address:

  • Email: martelespol@outlook.com
  • Contact form: [CONTACT FORM URL]

Write "Delete my account" and the account's email address. If you write from another address, we will ask you to write from the account's address, so that we know it is yours. We delete it within a maximum of one month (usually sooner) and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account and all its data are deleted, on our server and on the phone you delete it from:

  • Your email address, your name and your password (its fingerprint).
  • Your workouts, routines, own exercises, plans and rules.
  • Your coach answers, including health data if you gave it.
  • Your exercise suggestions.

We keep nothing afterwards. With the plan we use, our provider does not make database backups, so the deletion is permanent. Only the technical connection data (such as the IP address) remains for one day, and the provider deletes it automatically. If you used other phones with the same account, the encrypted copy kept by the app stays on them until you uninstall it or sign in with another account: if you like, uninstall the app on those phones.
