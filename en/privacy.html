ATHENA Privacy Policy

This policy is available in Spanish, English and German. All three versions are equally valid.

This policy explains what data ATHENA processes, why, where it is stored and what rights you have. We wrote it so that you can understand it without being a lawyer. If anything is unclear, write to us (see "Who is responsible").

Summary

  • To use ATHENA you need an account. Your data is stored in your account and synchronized between your phones.
  • An encrypted copy stays on your phone so that you can work out without a connection.
  • Health data (discomfort, injuries and areas with discomfort) is optional and is only stored in your account if you give your consent.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, no error reports sent to third parties and no external artificial intelligence. We do not sell or share your data.
  • Your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

Who is responsible

Controller for the processing of your data:

  • Pol Ciriano Marteles
  • Private individual resident in Germany
  • Email: martelespol@outlook.com
  • Contact form: https://tally.so/r/PRdPde

There is no data protection officer: given the size and type of the processing, the law does not require one.

Your account

When you create the account, we process:

  • Your email address and your password. We cannot see the password: only a fingerprint (hash) is stored, from which the password cannot be recovered.
  • Your name, which we use only to greet you in the app.
  • The date and the version of the terms of use that you accepted when you registered, so that we can prove that you accepted them.
  • A user ID and the dates of sign-up and sign-in.

Purpose: giving you access to the app and storing your data in your account. Legal basis: performance of the service you request from us (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

To send you the account emails (confirming your registration, the code to change your password and the notice about an unused account) we use the email service Brevo. Brevo receives your email address and the content of the message.

Your training data

It is stored in your account and synchronized between the phones you sign in on:

  • Your workouts: date, duration, sets, reps, weight of the load, perceived exertion (RPE) and any notes you write.
  • Your routines, your own exercises and your weekly plans.
  • Your coach rules ("My rules"): exercises you don't want to do and your settings. The areas with discomfort are treated separately (see "Health data").
  • The "My progress" suggestions that you have dismissed.

Purpose: so that you have your data on all your phones and don't lose it if you change phones or lose yours. Legal basis: performance of the service you request from us (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Workout notes are free text. They are not intended for health data. If you decide to write something about your health there, it will be stored like the rest of those answers or notes; you can delete it whenever you want.

When you delete something in the app (for example, a workout), its content is also deleted from your account. Only a marker without data remains, so that your other phones know that you deleted it. That marker disappears when you delete the account.

What stays only on your phone

  • A copy of your data, in an encrypted database, so that the app works without a connection. The changes you make without a connection are uploaded to your account when the connection returns.
  • The photos you choose from your gallery for an exercise. They are not uploaded to your account.
  • The app language and the reminders (they are chosen on each phone).
  • The workout you have in progress, until you save it.
  • The alerts for the end of a rest, warm-up or stretching and the reminders on training days: these are notifications scheduled by the phone itself, without going through any server.
  • The areas with discomfort, if you have not given the health consent.

When you sign out, the app first uploads the pending changes and then deletes all its data from that phone, except the language. What is in your account comes back when you sign in. The photos and the areas with discomfort that were only on that phone are lost (the app warns you beforehand).

Storing this data on your phone is necessary for the app to work as you ask, which is why we do not need to ask your permission separately (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG).

Data from before you had an account

If you used ATHENA without an account, the first time you sign in the app asks whether you want to save in your account the data you already had on the phone. It is not uploaded without asking you. If you choose not to save it, it is deleted from the phone. The areas with discomfort are only uploaded if you give the health consent.

Health data

The discomfort or injuries from the coach questionnaire and the areas with discomfort from "My rules" are health data. They are optional: you can use ATHENA and the coach without giving them.

They are only stored in your account if you tick the health consent box (in the Coach or in "My rules"). They serve only to avoid suggesting exercises that load those areas. Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We also store whether you gave that consent and when.

  • You can withdraw it at any time with the "Health data" switch (in the Coach or in "My rules"): your answers about discomfort or injuries and your areas with discomfort are deleted from your account. The areas remain on your phone.
  • Without consent, the areas with discomfort stay only on your phone. Our server does not accept them without it.
  • The health questionnaire is only for people aged 18 or over.

The coach

To suggest routines to you, the coach asks you to complete a questionnaire. Your answers are stored in your account.

General part: your goal, your level (for example, how many push-ups or pull-ups you can do in a row), the days and time you have, where you train, your equipment and your preferences. To store them we ask you to tick a box. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

There are two free-text answers: "Do you have a specific goal?" and "Is there anything you DON'T want to do?". They are not intended for health data. If you decide to write something about your health there, it will be stored like the rest of those answers or notes; you can delete it whenever you want.

With "Delete my coach data", all your answers are deleted from the server.

No automated decisions: the coach works with rules inside your phone and only suggests routines and changes to you. You decide whether to apply them. It does not make decisions with legal effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR).

Exercise suggestions

If you suggest an exercise for the catalog, we store the name, the types and the description you write, linked to your account, and the review status. The controller reviews them to decide whether to add the exercise to the shared catalog; if added, the exercise appears without any data about you.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request from us (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Exercise catalog

The app downloads the exercise catalog and the coach questionnaire from our server. These downloads do not send any data about you, except the technical connection data (such as the IP address).

Technical data

When the app connects to the server, our provider records technical data such as the IP address, date and time, so that the service works and is secure (for example, to stop attacks). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the security of the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you write to us

You can write to us by email or with the contact form on our website. We process your email address, what you write and the date, in order to reply. Legal basis: dealing with your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, if you exercise your rights, complying with our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

The form is on the Tally website, which stores the messages in the European Union. For the form to work, Tally uses technical session cookies; they are not used for advertising or tracking. The messages are not forwarded by email to any other service.

Our website with these texts is hosted on GitHub Pages. The website does not use cookies, analytics or content from other sites.

Who else processes your data

We work with three providers that process data on our behalf and only following our instructions, under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Supabase Inc. (USA): database, accounts and server. Your data is hosted in Frankfurt (Germany), on Amazon Web Services servers. Supabase is a United States company and some technical data could be processed there; these possible transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): sending the account emails, with servers in the European Union.
  • Tally (Tally BV, Belgium): the contact form on the website, with the messages stored in the European Union.

To download the app you use Google Play or the App Store. Google and Apple process your data as independent controllers, according to their own privacy policies. ATHENA does not include their analytics or advertising tools.

If you visit our website, GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA), which hosts it, records technical data such as your IP address for security purposes, as an independent controller according to its own privacy statement. These transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We do not give your data to anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep your data

  • In your account (account, training data, coach answers and suggestions): as long as you have the account. When you tap "Delete my account", they are deleted from the database at that moment. With the plan we use, the provider does not keep database backups, so no copies of your data remain on the server. If we ever turn them on, they will be deleted automatically within a maximum of 7 days and we will state this here.
  • Unused accounts: if you do not use your account for 24 months, we delete it with all its data, just like "Delete my account". Using the account means signing in, opening the app while signed in or uploading a change. We notify you by email 30 days beforehand; to keep it, simply open the app and sign in before the date in the notice. So that we do not notify you twice, we store the date of the notice, which is deleted when you use the account again or when it is deleted. Legal basis: not keeping data longer than necessary (Art. 5(1)(e) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in notifying you beforehand (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Health data: until you withdraw your consent, delete your coach data or delete your account.
  • On your phone: until you sign out, delete your account or uninstall the app.
  • Technical connection data: the provider deletes it automatically after 1 day.
  • Messages you send us: we delete them once your request has been dealt with. If you exercise your rights, we keep a record of the request and of our reply for 3 years, so that we can prove that we replied.
  • Email sending logs: for the limited time set by Brevo in its terms.

Security

  • Encrypted database on your phone, with the key stored in the system's secure storage.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) and data encrypted on the server.
  • Each user can only read and write their own data on the server (row-level security rules). No other user can see it.
  • Account password of at least 10 characters.
  • Your data is not end-to-end encrypted: technically, the controller and the server provider could access it. We do not do so unless you ask us to (for example, to help you with a problem) or the law requires us to.

Phone permissions

  • Photos (gallery): only to choose a photo for an exercise. The photo stays on your phone. On iPhone, the app also declares the camera and the microphone because a tool it uses requires it, but it does not use them.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: to tell you that a rest, a warm-up or a stretching session has ended, and to remind you on training days, even when the screen is off.
  • Start on boot (Android): so that scheduled reminders keep working after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for the alert at the end of a timer.
  • Keeping the screen on during the workout.
  • Internet: for the account, the synchronization, the coach and the catalog.

Minimum age

ATHENA is for people aged 16 or over. The coach's health questionnaire is for people aged 18 or over. You confirm this yourself when you create the account and when you give the health consent.

Are you obliged to give us your data?

To use ATHENA you need an account with your email address and a password: without them we cannot store or synchronize your data. The name is only used for the greeting. The coach questionnaire and the health data are optional: if you don't give health data, the coach works just the same, only it will not take your discomfort into account.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data and receive a copy.
  • Rectify data that is not correct.
  • Erase it (have us delete it).
  • Restrict its processing.
  • Portability: receive it in a format you can take elsewhere.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of the processing carried out before you withdrew it.

You can do many of these things yourself in the app:

  • View and correct your workouts, routines, name and coach answers.
  • Download all the data in your account as a JSON file, which can be opened with other programs (Settings ⚙️ → Account & cloud → Download my data). The file is not encrypted and may include health data: keep it in a safe place.
  • Delete your coach data or withdraw your health consent (Coach tab or "My rules").
  • Delete your account and all your data, in the cloud and on that phone (Settings ⚙️ → Account & cloud → Delete my account).

For anything else, or if you don't have the app, write to us at martelespol@outlook.com from your account's email address. We will reply within a maximum of one month.

Complaints

If you think we are not handling your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Ours is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59-61, 10555 Berlin, datenschutz-berlin.de). You can also lodge a complaint with the authority of the EU country where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change something important (for example, a new data item or a new provider), we will tell you in the app before it applies. The version and the date are at the beginning of this text.
