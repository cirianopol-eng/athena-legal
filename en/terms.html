What ATHENA is

ATHENA is an app for organizing and logging calisthenics and strength workouts: routines, weekly plans, set logging, statistics and a coach that suggests routines using simple rules.

  • The app is free.
  • To use it you need an account. Your data is stored in it and synchronized between your phones.

Health notice

ATHENA does not replace a doctor, a physiotherapist or a trainer.

  • If you have an injury or an illness, are pregnant, or have not exercised for a while, consult a doctor before you start.
  • Stop training if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or any unusual discomfort, and consult a professional.
  • The routines and suggestions from the coach are for guidance only. Adapt them to your level and do not do exercises that you do not know how to do safely.
  • ATHENA does not diagnose, treat or rehabilitate injuries. Marking an area with discomfort only serves so that the coach avoids suggesting exercises that load it.
